A collection of modifications for the game Ground of Aces

### Fireproofing
Tired of your brick roof catching on fire?  Should hangars catch fire like wood?  If your answer is NO, add Fireproofing to your buildables TODAY.

Buildables made fireproof will not ignite like wood, but still seem to be damaged by fire on adjacent terrain or objects, which leads to more expected fire damage to bombed structures.
